# Pubweb — Pricing Tiers

## Setup Fees

| | Starter | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One-time setup** | £150 | £200 | £300 |

Setup includes: initial site build, domain configuration, data entry (menus, hours, images), admin account creation, and onboarding walkthrough.

> **Launch offer**: Setup fee waived for selected local businesses as we grow the platform.

---

## Annual Plans

### Starter — £300/year

A clean, professional single-page website. Perfect for smaller cafes, takeaways, and local eateries who need an online presence without the complexity.

**Includes:**
- Single-page site with business name, address, opening hours, phone number, and social media links
- Google Maps embed
- Mobile-responsive design
- Admin access to update hours and contact info
- Hosting and SSL included

---

### Standard — £600/year

A full multi-page website with everything a busy restaurant or pub needs to showcase their food and venue.

**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- Homepage with hero image carousel, welcome section, and highlights
- Menus page — upload menu images or build menus with items, prices, and dietary tags
- Gallery page with image uploads and captions
- Dedicated contact page with map, hours, and special closure notices
- Events/special menus support with date ranges and booking links
- Full admin panel — manage menus, gallery, hours, and venue info
- Cloudinary image hosting

---

### Premium — £900/year

The complete package for venues with accommodation, multiple offerings, or bespoke content needs.

**Includes everything in Standard, plus:**
- Accommodation page with room listings, features, images, and booking links
- Local attractions/explore page
- Custom content pages (charity initiatives, buffet services, etc.)
- Award and accreditation showcases
- Full admin panel including room management, attractions, and custom page editing
- Priority support
