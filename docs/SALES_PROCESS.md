# Online Front Door — Sales Process

A systematic pipeline for converting local businesses into paying customers. Process-driven, measurable, repeatable.

## Principles

1. **The site does the selling.** Every prospect gets a working preview before any conversation happens.
2. **Every touch adds value.** Never "just checking in" — every contact includes an update, a stat, or useful information.
3. **Measure everything.** Log every action, track conversion at each stage, identify bottlenecks.
4. **Batch the work.** Build 5-10 preview sites, then do a delivery round. Don't context-switch between building and selling.
5. **Process, not personality.** This should work the same whether it's you, a staff member, or a commission rep.

## Pipeline

### Stage 0 — FIND

**Action:** Identify businesses that need a website and don't have one.

**Where to look:**
- Facebook — active business page, no website linked or dead link
- Google Maps — listed but no website, or website is broken/outdated
- Walking around — signage, menus in windows, vans with a phone number but no URL
- Word of mouth — people mention a place, you check if they're online

**What makes a good prospect:**
- Active business (posting on Facebook, open on Google, clearly trading)
- No website, or a website so bad it's effectively nothing
- Enough public info to build a preview (name, location, hours, photos, menu/services)
- Contactable — you can drop in, message on Facebook, or find an email

**What to capture:**
- Business name, type, location
- Facebook page URL (if any)
- Google listing URL (if any)
- Contact method (drop in, Facebook message, email, phone)
- Notes on what content is available to build from

**Exit criteria:** Prospect logged in tracker, enough info gathered to build a preview.

---

### Stage 1 — BUILD

**Action:** Create a preview site for the prospect.

- Use the info gathered in FIND to build the site
- Build the site at `[business].onlinefrontdoor.co.uk`
- Register/claim their Google Business Profile if not already done
- Target: under 1 hour per site once the template is mature (currently longer — gets faster with each build)

**Exit criteria:** Live preview site with real business info.

---

### Stage 2 — DELIVER

**Action:** Get the leaflet and preview URL to the business.

- Generic leaflet + personalised handwritten note with their URL
- Drop at the business in person
- Log: date, who received it, any immediate reaction

**Exit criteria:** Leaflet delivered, logged.

---

### Stage 3 — CHECK (Week 1-2)

**Action:** Confirm they've seen the preview.

- Review analytics: did they visit?
- If yes → move to ENGAGE
- If no visit after 2 weeks → pop in casually, mention the site
- Log: visited (yes/no), date, method of check

**Exit criteria:** Confirmed they've seen the site, or second prompt delivered.

---

### Stage 4 — ENGAGE

**Action:** Make contact and add value.

- Make one visible improvement to their preview (updated photo, seasonal change, corrected hours)
- Let them know: "I've updated your preview — have a look"
- Ask what they think, note any feedback
- Share any stats if available (visits, Google impressions)
- Log: what was updated, their response, any requests

**Exit criteria:** Two-way conversation started.

---

### Stage 5 — IMPROVE (Week 3-5)

**Action:** Iterate on their preview based on feedback.

- Apply any changes they've asked for
- Show them the result
- Ask: "Is there anything else you'd want it to do?"
- Share updated stats — "X people looked at your site this week"
- This shifts from "buy this" to "help me build this for you"
- Log: changes made, response, engagement level

**Exit criteria:** Prospect has seen at least two iterations of their site.

---

### Stage 6 — CONVERT (Week 5-7)

**Action:** Make the offer clear.

- "Ready to make it official? £55/month, or £600 for the year. Cancel anytime."
- If yes → onboard
- If "I'll think about it" → set a follow-up date, give a soft deadline: "The preview stays live for another month — no rush"
- If no → move to PAUSED
- Log: outcome, next action date, any objections

**Exit criteria:** Signed up, given a deadline, or moved to PAUSED.

---

### Stage 7 — PAUSED

**Action:** Quarterly maintenance.

- Not dead — circumstances change (new manager, agency frustration, competitor goes online)
- Every 3 months: make a small update to their preview, mention it
- "I noticed your hours changed on Google — I've updated your preview to match"
- Log: date, what was updated, any response

**Exit criteria:** Re-enters ENGAGE if they respond, or stays in PAUSED.

---

## Stage Limits

| Stage | Max dwell time | If exceeded |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| FIND | — | Ongoing, batch when you have 3-5 |
| BUILD | — | Batched, not time-limited |
| DELIVER | 1 week from build | Deliver or deprioritise |
| CHECK | 2 weeks | Second prompt, then ENGAGE |
| ENGAGE | 2 weeks | IMPROVE or PAUSED |
| IMPROVE | 3 weeks | CONVERT |
| CONVERT | 2 weeks | PAUSED |
| PAUSED | Indefinite | Quarterly check-in |

### Stage definitions

- **FIND** — Prospect identified, gathering info
- **BUILD** — Preview site being created
- **DELIVER** — Leaflet/outreach delivered, awaiting response
- **CHECK** — Checking whether they've seen the preview
- **ENGAGE** — In conversation, showing value
- **IMPROVE** — Iterating on their preview based on feedback
- **CONVERT** — Offer made, awaiting decision
- **PAUSED** — Quiet, quarterly check-in only
- **PAYING** — Live customer
- **CLOSED** — Explicitly declined, no further action

## Metrics

After every batch of 10 prospects, review:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Preview visit rate | Is the leaflet/note working? |
| DELIVER → ENGAGE rate | Are they interested once they see it? |
| ENGAGE → CONVERT rate | Is the product convincing? |
| Average days to convert | How long is the sales cycle? |
| Conversion by business type | Where to focus next |
| Common objections | What to address in the leaflet/pitch |

## B2B2B Channel

A parallel acquisition channel through business-to-business partnerships.

**Partners:** Accountants, printers, business suppliers — anyone who already serves multiple local businesses.

**Two models:**

| Model | Partner gets | We get | Best for |
|-------|-------------|--------|----------|
| **Revenue share** | £15/month per referral for 3 years | £40/month from day 1 | Partners who want passive income |
| **White label** | Resells at their price, pays us £15/month per site | £15/month guaranteed, zero sales effort | Partners who want to offer it as their own service |

**Process:**
1. Identify partners who serve 20+ local businesses
2. Pitch the partnership: "Your clients need this, you earn from it"
3. Partner introduces us or provides a client list
4. We build previews for their clients
5. Standard pipeline takes over from DELIVER stage

This channel runs alongside direct sales — it doesn't replace it.

## Rep Handoff

When commission reps are in place:

- You handle BUILD (site creation)
- Rep handles DELIVER → CONVERT (the legwork)
- Rep can request preview improvements (you or staff member fulfils)
- Same process, same logging, same rules
- Rep earns 50% of first year revenue per conversion (£330)
